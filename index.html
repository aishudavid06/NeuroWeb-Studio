01 THE PROBLEM

A polished screen can still be a broken experience.

Frontend issues often hide behind a good-looking layout: unlabeled inputs, images without alternatives, buttons with no accessible name, repeated IDs, or interactions that fail silently. These problems are easy to miss when a page is judged only by appearance.

↳THE GAP: visual design is visible. Interface quality needs to be tested.
